99.99% Virgin Lead Alloy

While low-cost batteries rely on recycled lead oxide that sulphates quickly, Inva Power uses primary electrolytic virgin lead with selenium-antimony chemistry to prevent grid corrosion.

High Pressure Spine Casting (100 Bar)

Our tall tubular inverter batteries are manufactured using 100 bar high pressure die casting. This eliminates micro-voids, providing up to 1,500 discharge cycles at 80% DOD.

Vibration-Proof Poly-Ribbed Case

The Express truck and Jai Kisan tractor series feature heavy-duty PP copolymer ribbed casings designed to withstand severe agricultural and mining vibrations without shedding active plate material.
